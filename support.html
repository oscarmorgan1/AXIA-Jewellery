Shipping

Every AXIA piece is hand-set and made to order. Allow approximately 3-5 weeks for your piece to be made before it ships express, tracked and insured worldwide. The shipping windows below apply once your piece leaves our workshop.

ExpressCalculated at checkout · 1-3 business days
StandardCalculated at checkout · 3-6 days
InternationalCalculated at checkout · 5-12 days

International duties and taxes may apply on delivery and are the recipient's responsibility.

Returns & exchanges

Changed your mind, or the size isn't right? You have 30 days from delivery.

  • Pieces must be unworn and in original condition with packaging and cards.
  • Size exchanges are free, we cover return postage both ways.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the piece is inspected.
  • Custom or engraved pieces can't be returned unless faulty.

To start, email axiajewellery@gmail.com with your order number and we'll send a prepaid label. Or use the contact form below.

Warranty

Every AXIA piece carries a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects: clasp failure, stone loss from a setting, or finishing faults.

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

  • Covered: manufacturing and material defects under normal wear.
  • Not covered: general wear, scratching, loss, or damage from misuse (impact, chemicals, swimming, gym).
  • We'll repair or replace at our discretion; keep your order confirmation as proof of purchase.

Sizing

Chains and bracelets come in a range of lengths. If you're between sizes, size up.

Bracelets7″ - 9″ (18-23 cm)
Chains16″ - 24″ (41-61 cm)
Between sizes?Go longer for an easier fit

To check: measure a chain or bracelet you already own end to end, or lay a piece of string where you'd want it to sit and measure it against a ruler. Every piece lists its exact available lengths on its product page, and size exchanges are free.

Care

  • Wipe with the included polishing cloth after wear.
  • Remove before swimming, showering, sleeping and the gym to protect the finish.
  • Keep away from perfume, chlorine and harsh cleaners.
  • Store flat in the pouch, away from other metals.

A care card is included with every order.

Materials & moissanite

We lead with design and tell you exactly what a piece is made of. The precise specification is printed on every product tag.

Metal925 sterling silver · white-gold plated
StoneVVS D-colour moissanite · passes a diamond tester
SettingHand-set · pavé, prong & clasp editions

On moissanite: it's a brilliant, hard, ethically created stone we chose for how it throws light and how it wears. It is not a diamond, and we'd never pretend otherwise. We use it because it lets the design carry the piece at a price that makes sense, not to fool anyone.

Packaging & cases

Every AXIA order ships in an AXIA box. Not a bubble mailer with a sticker on it. A proper box, with a pouch and a care card, so your piece turns up the way it should and has somewhere to live between wears. That is included with everything, at no extra cost.

For the pieces you travel with, or once you own a few, we are bringing out AXIA travel cases: soft-lined and zip-close, built to hold a stack of chains and bracelets without them tangling or scratching. Travel cases are sold separately from the jewellery.
