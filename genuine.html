Why there's only one

Your serial isn't a product code. It belongs to the piece in your hand and nothing else, and the record behind it lives with us.

  1. 01

    One serial

    Each piece is logged in the AXIA registry under its own number, matched to the card in its box. No two pieces share one.

  2. 02

    One owner

    The record is tied to the order it was bought in. Hand it on through your account and the record moves with it, so it only ever has one owner at a time.

  3. 03

    One record

    Check it here, any time. If the piece named on the record isn't the one in front of you, it isn't ours.
