Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

AXIA Jewellery (ABN 64 972 889 034), based in Canberra, ACT, Australia ("AXIA", "we", "us") runs axiajewellery.com (axiajewellery.com.au redirects there) and its customer account area. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can access, correct or delete it. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

At a glance

  • We collect what you give us (to join the waitlist, buy, create an account or contact us) and a little about how the site is used.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.
  • Marketing emails go only to people who opted in, and every one has an unsubscribe link.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it: contact us.

1. What we collect

When you join the waitlist (First Access)

Your first name, email address, mobile number, Instagram handle (optional), the piece and size you're interested in, whether you agreed to emails and to text messages, which page you signed up on, and when. We also send you a welcome email with a first-order code, and keep a record that it was sent.

When you place an order

Checkout runs on Stripe. From Stripe we receive and keep your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, the pieces you ordered (with sizes and any engraving text), the delivery option, any promo code, the amounts paid, and the order's status as it moves from paid to shipped. We also keep a customer record under your email address that links your orders together, with your latest contact details and any notes we add to look after you (for example, about a resize or a delivery request).

When you have a customer account

If you create or claim an account (with an email and password, or with Google), we keep your email address and sign-in details. We never see your password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and, where Google provides it, your profile photo. Your account holds your name, phone, address, email preferences and the items saved in your bag, plus copies of your orders, pieces and conversations with us so you can see them.

Pieces, transfers, returns and exchanges

  • Piece registry. Each piece has a serial number linked to the order it was sold in and its owner. The public authenticity check at /genuine only shows the piece and its purchase date, never who owns it.
  • Transfers. If you transfer a piece to someone, we keep their email address, any note you add, and the outcome. We email them an invitation to accept it, and keep the piece's ownership history.
  • Returns and exchanges. The pieces involved, your reason and notes, the return label and tracking, any refund or replacement, and each step's date.

When you contact us

Messages from the support form or your account: your name, email address, topic, order number (if you give one) and your message, plus our replies.

Emails we send

We send emails through Resend. For each email we keep what was sent, to whom and when. For marketing emails, Resend tells us whether each was delivered, opened or clicked, and whether it bounced or was marked as spam, so we can measure campaigns and stop emailing addresses that don't want them.

While you browse

  • Visitor count. Our site keeps a random ID in your browser and records one visit per day: the page you landed on, the website that referred you, and whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer. It contains nothing that identifies you.
  • Google Analytics, explained in section 5.
  • Security checks. Google reCAPTCHA checks that requests come from a real browser on our site. To do this Google collects information about your device and browser and how you interact with the page, under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Server logs. Like any website, our servers briefly process your IP address and browser details to deliver pages, limit abuse (for example, too many sign-ups from one address) and diagnose errors. Our hosting provider keeps these logs for a limited time.

We don't collect sensitive information (such as health or financial information beyond what a payment needs) and ask that you don't send it to us. You can browse without telling us who you are; you'll need to give contact and delivery details to buy.

2. How we use it

  • To take, make, engrave, ship and support your order, including returns, exchanges, warranty and refunds.
  • To run your customer account, the piece registry, authenticity checks and transfers.
  • To reply to your messages and support requests.
  • To send order and account emails (confirmations, shipping updates, sign-in links and invitations). These aren't marketing and you'll receive them while you have an order or account with us.
  • To send launch news, early access, offers and new drops, only if you opted in (see Marketing).
  • To run promotions such as the First Drop giveaway, as set out in our giveaway terms.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To keep the site, our customers and our business secure, prevent fraud, and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Storage outside Australia

Some of our providers store or process information outside Australia, mainly in the United States, and may use data centres in other countries where they operate (including the European Union). This includes Stripe, Resend, Google Analytics and Google reCAPTCHA, and parts of Google’s hosting (such as account sign-in). We choose providers with strong security and privacy practices, and take reasonable steps under APP 8 to make sure they handle your information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

5. Analytics, cookies and browser storage

On our public pages we use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site. It doesn't run in your customer account area, on checkout pages, or in our admin tools, and we don't use it for advertising. We don't record your visits or use heatmap tools.

  • Google Analytics tells us which pages are viewed, how people move between them and where they leave, which websites and campaigns bring visitors, the type of device and browser, and approximate location (country and city, worked out from your IP address, which Google doesn't store). It sets first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*). We've turned off Google signals and ad personalisation, and we strip anything you've typed or any serial number from page addresses before they're sent.

Analytics runs when you visit unless you decline. A short notice lets you choose the first time you visit, and you can change your mind here at any time. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a decline.

Your analytics choice: On.

You can also block or clear cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Browser storage. To make the site work, we keep a few things in your own browser's storage (not cookies): your bag and when it last changed, a copy of your waitlist sign-up so the page remembers you've joined, pieces you've shown interest in, display preferences (such as the shop grid), your analytics choice, and the random ID used for the visitor count. If you have an account, your browser also remembers your sign-in so you stay signed in. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of these.

6. Marketing

We send launch news, early access, offers and new drops only to people who ticked the box to receive them when joining the waitlist, or turned on "New drops and offers" in their account. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link (one click, no sign-in), and marking one as spam also unsubscribes you. You can also switch emails off in your account, or ask us. If you agreed to text messages, we may text you launch alerts. We don't send texts yet; when we do, your mobile number will go to the SMS provider we use, and we'll name it in this policy. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Unsubscribing doesn't stop emails about an order or your account.

7. How long we keep it

  • Orders, payments and the customer record that goes with them: for at least five years after the order, as Australian tax law requires, and while the piece's warranty and registry record are useful to you.
  • Customer accounts: until you ask us to delete yours.
  • Waitlist sign-ups and marketing preferences: until you ask us to delete them. If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a do-not-email list so we don't contact you again.
  • Support messages: while we need them to look after you and your order.
  • Account invitation links expire after 30 days and are deleted once used. Transfer offers expire after 14 days.
  • Visitor counts are kept as anonymous statistics.
  • Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. It is stored with Google, encrypted, and only the AXIA team can see customer details. No system is perfectly secure, but if a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

9. Access, correction and deletion

You can see and update most of your details in your account. You can also ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We'll confirm who you are first (usually by replying from the email address we hold), then respond within 30 days. When we delete your information, we remove your orders and account from our systems, along with your messages, waitlist sign-ups, invitations, transfers and your name in piece ownership history, and remove you from our email provider. Payment records held by Stripe, and records we must keep by law, may be kept for as long as the law requires.

10. Contact and complaints

For any privacy question, request or complaint, email support@axiajewellery.com or use our contact form. Our address is AXIA Jewellery, Canberra ACT, Australia.

We'll respond to a complaint within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business or the law changes. We'll post the new version here with its date, and tell customers by email about any significant change.
