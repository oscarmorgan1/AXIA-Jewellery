The material

925 silver. Hand-set moissanite.

Every AXIA piece is solid 925 sterling silver, plated in white gold to stay bright and resist tarnish. Not plated brass. Not steel pretending to be silver. The metal is real, it has weight, and the exact spec is printed on every tag.

The stones are VVS clarity, D-colour moissanite, hand-set one at a time. Moissanite is a genuinely hard, brilliant stone that throws more fire than a diamond, and ours passes a diamond tester. The difference is the price: a diamond version of one of these chains runs well into five figures. We would rather you wear this one every day and never think twice about it.

We will always tell you exactly what it is. We are not selling you a diamond and hoping you don't ask.
